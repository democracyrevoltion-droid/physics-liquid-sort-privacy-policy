Privacy Policy for Physics Liquid Sort

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android app Physics Liquid Sort (shown in the game as "Liquid Physics Sort"), published on Google Play by vinfoxgame ("we", "us", "our"). It explains what information the app collects, uses, and shares.

1. Information We Collect

Physics Liquid Sort does not collect any personal or sensitive user data. We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, advertising ID, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, or any other personal information.

The app works fully offline and does not require an account or an internet connection.

2. Information We Share

Because we do not collect any data, we do not share, sell, rent, or transfer any user data to any third party.

3. Data Stored Only on Your Device

The app saves your game progress (current level) and your sound settings (sound effects and music volume) locally on your own device. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is removed when you uninstall the app or use the in-game "Reset Progress" button.

4. Permissions

The app does not request any sensitive Android permissions.

5. Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking services, and contains no ads or in-app purchases.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Since no personal data is collected, the app can be used by people of all ages.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We hold no user data on any server. To delete the data stored on your device, uninstall the app or use "Reset Progress" in the in-game settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
